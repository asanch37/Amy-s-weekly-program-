Program Overview

Structure (5–6 training days / week):

  • Day 1 – Legs A (Quad-dominant + posterior)
  • Day 2 – Shoulders
  • Day 3 – Chest & Back
  • Day 4 – Legs B (Posterior + unilateral)
  • Day 5 – Arms
  • Day 6 – Long-form CrossFit / Benchmark (no supersets)
  • Day 7 – Rest or optional light mobility

Progression: Supersets increase in intensity, volume, or complexity every 2 weeks (4-week cycles). Load should feel challenging in the last 2–3 reps of each set.

Finishers: Most strength days end with a short AMRAP, EMOM, or chipper (8–15 min).

Long CrossFit days: Pure benchmark or mixed-modal workouts (Fran, Helen, Cindy, Murph-style, etc.) once per week.

Warm-up: 5–7 min every session (general + specific).
